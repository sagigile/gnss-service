# Provenance

`legacy_main.py` is the GNSS solver from the NaviProject coursework repository
(https://github.com/sagigile/NaviProject), authored by Sagi Rahat and Taliya Levin.
It is included here unmodified; `pipeline.py` wraps it as a library.

The sample RINEX files in `tests/data/` (`my_data_*.obs`, `samsung_nav.nav.rnx`) and the reference output
`golden_clean_short_path.csv` also come from that repository.
